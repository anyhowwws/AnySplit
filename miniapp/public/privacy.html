AnySplit — Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

AnySplit is a Telegram bot that splits a restaurant bill from a photo of the receipt. This policy describes exactly what it receives, what it keeps, and for how long. It replaces Telegram's Standard Bot Privacy Policy for this bot.

The short version. Your receipt photo is never stored. The bill is deleted 24 hours after you last touch it. Phone numbers are never stored at all. A running count of how many people use the bot is kept against a hashed ID that cannot be traced back to you. There are no accounts, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

1. Who runs this

AnySplit is operated by an individual developer, not a company, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram. Contact details are in section 8.

2. What AnySplit receives

  • The receipt photo you send to the bot.
  • Your Telegram user ID, supplied by Telegram so the bot can reply to you and can tell your bill apart from someone else's.
  • Names you type for the people you're splitting with. These are free text — they can be nicknames, initials, or anything you like.
  • Optionally, a name and phone number for whoever should be paid back, if you choose to enter one.

AnySplit does not ask for, and cannot see, your phone number, contact list, other chats, or any Telegram data beyond the message you send it.

3. What is stored, and for how long

DataStored?Kept for
Receipt photo Never stored Held in memory only while being read, then discarded
Payee phone number Never stored Used only to write your messages, then discarded
Bill contents — merchant, items, prices, the names you entered, each person's share Yes, temporarily Deleted automatically 24 hours after you last work on it. Sending doesn't end it, so a split can still be corrected or re-sent
Operational logs — timings, error messages, counts Yes 14 days
A usage tally — a hashed ID, when it was first and last seen, and how many receipts it has sent Yes, indefinitely Kept until you ask for it to be removed. It holds no bills, names, merchants or amounts

Your Telegram user ID is never stored as itself. Everywhere it would appear — in the logs and in the usage tally — it is replaced by an irreversible keyed hash, so both can answer how many people use this bot without being able to say who they are. The hash cannot be turned back into your ID, or matched to you by anyone who obtained it.

Logs do not contain your receipt, your item names, any phone number, or the text of your messages. The usage tally is a count and two dates; it records that a pseudonym used the bot, never what it was used for.

4. Reading the receipt

To turn a photo into a list of items, AnySplit sends the image to Anthropic's Claude API. This is the only place your photo goes, and it is sent for this single purpose. Anthropic's handling of it is governed by their own privacy policy.

The bot's infrastructure runs on Amazon Web Services in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) region. Messages are delivered by Telegram and are subject to Telegram's privacy policy.

5. Phone numbers you enter for someone else

If you enter a phone number so people know who to pay back, that number is used only to write it into the messages you forward, and is never saved. Please only enter someone else's number if they are happy for you to share it with the people you're splitting with.

6. What AnySplit never does

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no password.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics tied to you.
  • Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with anyone beyond the services in section 4.
  • No payment is taken or processed — AnySplit only tells people what they owe.
  • No record is kept of past bills, who you eat with, or what you order.
  • The usage tally in section 3 counts receipts; it never records what was on them.

7. Your rights

You can ask for a copy of any personal data held about you, or ask for it to be deleted. Requests are answered within 30 days.

In practice there is very little to send: a bill is gone within 24 hours of your last action on it, and the operational logs in section 3 delete themselves within 14 days. What persists is the usage tally — a hashed ID, two dates and a number. Ask and it will be deleted; because it is only ever reached through that hash, removing it removes everything held about you.

You can stop using AnySplit at any time by deleting the chat and blocking the bot. Your bills and logs age out on their own; the usage tally stays until you ask for it to go, so if you want nothing left behind, say so using the contact address below.
